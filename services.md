# Clinic Services

This is fictional clinic information for a coursework project.

## Available Services

- General health consultations
- Preventive health education
- Routine follow-up appointments

## Appointment Requests

Patients can request a call-back to discuss an appointment.
A call-back request does not confirm a booking.

## Intake Forms

Patients can request an intake form before their appointment.
This coursework repository does not collect patient information.
